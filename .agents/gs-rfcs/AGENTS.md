# AGENTS.md — GS-RFCs

English | [中文](AGENTS.zh.md)

GS-RFCs are grill-me-sleek's RFCs: durable proposals and decision records preserving the why, the alternatives that lost, and what code and specs cannot carry. The full contract lives in the [GS-RFC rules](README.md); the repo [documentation standard](../../specs/AGENTS.md) governs every document.

**Every new record triggers a duplicate check** ([scope](README.md#when-to-write-one)): the decision may already have a home in this tree.

**Never edit a record into a different decision** — supersede it with a new GS-RFC and cross-link both ([lifecycle rules](README.md#layout-and-naming)).

Edit `README.md` and `README.zh.md` together; every record pairs with a `.zh.md` twin the same way ([documentation standard](../../specs/AGENTS.md)).
